Learn SRE and platform engineering well enough to teach them

This course walks you through two books, one lesson per chapter: all 23 chapters of Site Reliability Engineering, 2nd Edition, and the 10 chapters published so far of Platform Engineering at Google. I’m your tutor: each lesson explains the ideas in plain words, shows them as a picture, and then makes you practise saying them the way you would in an interview. Everything is written in my own words and mapped to AWS.

Every lesson follows the same pattern
  • The big picture: the chapter’s argument in short paragraphs, with a diagram.
  • Going deeper: the case studies, numbers and named models interviewers probe for.
  • Say it in an interview, the AWS translation, and a question with a model answer you reveal after trying.
  • A quick check, an exercise (often a POC to run from the repo) and three takeaways.

Mark a lesson complete at the bottom and its station on the route map fills in. Your progress is kept in this browser.

How to study each lesson
  • Read it once, then close it and explain it out loud without looking.
  • Answer the interview question aloud before you open the model answer.
  • Do the exercise. Running the code is what makes the ideas stick.
